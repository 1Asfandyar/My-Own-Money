# Data Processing Addendum (Controller Notice)

Effective Date: 2026-07-19  
Version: 1.0

This document supplements the Privacy Policy and Terms for My Own Money ("MOM - My Own Money"). It reflects the current product model where users are individual consumers and the App operator acts primarily as an independent controller of personal data for service delivery.

Owners/Operators: Abdur Rahman and Asfandyar  
Contact: khalil.ahmad1547@gmail.com

## 1. Purpose

This addendum describes:
- data processing roles,
- categories of personal data,
- processing purposes,
- security and subprocessor approach,
- user rights and deletion handling.

## 2. Roles

- App Operators: independent data controllers for data processed to operate and secure the App.
- End Users: data subjects and content submitters.

No controller-to-processor enterprise DPA is currently offered because the service is not currently sold as a B2B processor platform. If enterprise plans launch, a separate controller-processor DPA can be published.

## 3. Categories of Personal Data

- account identifiers (email),
- authentication data (password hash and auth metadata),
- finance and ledger entries submitted by users,
- invite emails and shared-entry relationships,
- crash/error logs,
- push notification tokens.

## 4. Processing Purposes

- account creation and authentication,
- operation of personal/shared finance features,
- invite and collaboration workflows,
- service reliability and troubleshooting,
- security and abuse prevention,
- legal compliance.

## 5. Subprocessors and Infrastructure

Current/expected infrastructure includes cloud hosting providers (currently Render; future migration may include DigitalOcean or AWS). We may engage additional subprocessors for operations and delivery as needed.

We require service providers to process data under confidentiality and appropriate safeguards.

## 6. International Data Transfers

Where data is transferred across borders, we use reasonable safeguards appropriate for the scale and risk profile of the service.

## 7. Retention and Deletion

Active user data is retained while accounts are active and for functionality needs. Deletion requests remove or de-identify personal linkage without undue delay, except where limited retention is necessary for legal obligations, security, dispute handling, or backup lifecycle.

## 8. Security Measures (Baseline)

Security measures are risk-based and may evolve, including:
- transport encryption where supported,
- access controls and credential protection,
- environment-level safeguards by hosting providers,
- logging/monitoring for abuse and reliability.

No specific certification (for example, ISO 27001 or SOC 2) is currently claimed.

## 9. Data Subject Requests

Requests for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or consent withdrawal (where applicable) may be submitted to: khalil.ahmad1547@gmail.com

## 10. Incident Response

We will investigate suspected personal data incidents and provide notices where required by applicable law.

## 11. Updates

We may update this addendum as product scope, laws, and infrastructure evolve.
