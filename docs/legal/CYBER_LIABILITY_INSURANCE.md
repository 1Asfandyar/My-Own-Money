## 1. Current Status

My Own Money does not currently maintain a standalone cyber liability insurance policy.

## 2. Commitment

As the product grows, we intend to evaluate and obtain suitable cyber coverage aligned with risk, user base, and commercial requirements.

## 3. Target Coverage Areas (Roadmap)

When sourcing a policy, target protections should include:
- incident response and forensic investigation,
- data breach notification and related response costs,
- privacy and network security liability,
- media liability (if relevant),
- cyber extortion/ransomware response,
- business interruption and recovery costs,
- third-party claims and legal defense.

## 4. Security and Risk Management Alignment

Insurance is intended to complement, not replace, technical and organizational safeguards.

## 5. Public Communication

If insurance coverage is obtained, this statement can be updated with:
- carrier name,
- policy period,
- high-level coverage scope,
- contact path for verified business due diligence requests.
