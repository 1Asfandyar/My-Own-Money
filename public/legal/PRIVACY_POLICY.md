## 1. Scope

This Policy applies to personal data processed through the App.

## 2. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of data:
- Account Data: email address, password hash, authentication metadata.
- User Content: personal finance entries, shared finance entries, account/category data, and related notes you enter.
- Shared/Invite Data: invitee email addresses and shared record links.
- App Reliability Data: crash logs and error diagnostics.
- Notifications Data: push notification tokens and delivery metadata.

We do not intentionally store full IP logs in our application data model. However, infrastructure providers may process technical logs (which can include IP addresses) for security and operations.

## 3. How We Use Data

We use personal data to:
- create and manage accounts,
- provide personal and shared finance tracking features,
- send operational notifications,
- detect and fix reliability/security issues,
- maintain service integrity and prevent abuse,
- comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Legal Bases (where applicable)

Depending on your jurisdiction, we process personal data based on:
- performance of a contract (providing the App),
- legitimate interests (security, service improvement),
- consent (where required, such as certain notifications),
- legal obligations.

## 5. Data Sharing

We share data only as needed with:
- hosting/infrastructure providers (currently Render; may migrate to DigitalOcean or AWS),
- technical service providers supporting operation, security, and delivery.

We do not sell personal data.

## 6. International Processing

Your data may be processed in countries where our providers operate. We take reasonable contractual and technical steps to protect data during such transfers.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

If you delete data, we delete it from active systems without undue delay.

If you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your personal account linkage. Shared transaction records involving other users may remain for ledger consistency, but where feasible they will no longer be linked to your identifiable account profile.

Some residual copies may remain briefly in secure backups before routine overwrite.

## 8. Security

We apply reasonable safeguards appropriate to a small, evolving product. No method of storage or transmission is fully secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13. If we learn we collected personal data from a child under 13 without required authorization, we will delete it.
